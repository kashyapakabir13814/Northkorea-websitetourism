General Information

Capital: Pyongyang — a city of monumental architecture, wide boulevards, and cultural landmarks.

Currency: North Korean Won (KPW) — used for local transactions, though foreign visitors often rely on euros or Chinese yuan.

Population: Approximately 25 million people, with a strong emphasis on collective identity and cultural heritage.

North Korea offers a unique glimpse into a society shaped by history, ideology, and tradition. Visitors encounter striking monuments, scenic landscapes, and a culture deeply rooted in resilience and pride.
