Juche Tower

A towering monument in Pyongyang, built to celebrate the philosophy of self-reliance. From the top, you can see the city spread out in every direction.

Kim Il-sung Square

The central square of Pyongyang, famous for its parades and national celebrations. Standing here gives you a sense of the country’s scale and pride.

Mount Paektu

A volcanic mountain on the border with China, home to the stunning Heaven Lake. It’s considered sacred and is one of the most beautiful natural sights in Korea.
